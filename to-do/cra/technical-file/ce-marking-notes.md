# CE marking notes (draft; CE not affixed by this scaffold)

**Explicit status:** This repository scaffold does **not** claim that CE marking has been affixed to CPN. Do not state "CE marked" in README, release notes, download pages, or marketing until the manufacturer signatory authorises it after conformity assessment.

## What CE marking will require (when authorised)

1. Completed Annex VII technical documentation for the version range placed on the market.
2. Completed conformity assessment path appropriate to the final classification (harmonised standards and/or notified body for Important Class I).
3. Signed EU Declaration of Conformity retained by the manufacturer.
4. CE mark affixed in accordance with CRA / Blue Guide practice for software products (location and accompanying documentation per counsel).
5. Manufacturer name, address, and contact available with the product / download materials.

## Forbidden until authorisation

- Embedding a CE logo in the panel UI, installer, or site as a conformity claim
- Shipping a "signed DoC" PDF generated by CI without a human signatory
- Implying CE conformity in Discord/changelog announcements
