## Rules for this folder

1. Do not place secrets, private keys, ENISA credentials, or insurance policy numbers here.
2. Do not mark products as CE-affixed in README, release notes, or marketing until the legal track completes authorisation.
3. Keep drafts clearly labelled **DRAFT / UNSIGNED**.
4. Point to living evidence under `to-do/cra/` and product docs rather than duplicating large logs in git.
